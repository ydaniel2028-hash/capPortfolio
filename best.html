My Best Work

The game I developed is about a shark hunting for fish that fishermen release back into the ocean. The player controls the shark and tries to catch the good fish to increase their score while avoiding toxic fish that lower the shark’s health. If the shark eats a poisonous fish, the health bar goes down, and the player loses when their health reaches zero. The goal of the game is to survive long enough to catch at least 8 good fish and win. I included moving fish, score tracking, health displays, and win or lose screens to make the game fun and challenging. Although I have many projects I am proud of, this definitely takes the win. I greatly enjoyed creating this and I think that is what made it so great.This game is one of my best works because it shows creativity, problem-solving, and programming skills all working together. I designed a unique underwater survival game where players must make quick decisions by choosing between safe and toxic fish. The project demonstrates my ability to create game mechanics such as movement, collision detection, scoring systems, health tracking, and win/lose conditions. It also shows that I can plan and organize a complete piece of software from start to finish. By combining gameplay, visuals, and coding, this project shows both my technical skills and my creativity as a game designer.
