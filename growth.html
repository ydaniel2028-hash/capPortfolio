My Growth

In this project I defined a problem that could be addressed by a recommendation, identify the data you need, created a survey to collect your data, interpreted the data to find relationships between survey answers, then created an algorithm to make a prediction based on data, tested my algorithm, and created a recommendation app that uses my algorithm to make a recommendation for a user. I created a buzzfeed quiz that helps you identify which Stranger Things character you are. The main question was “What Stranger Things character are you based on your personality, your style, and the quote that resonates most with you?” The results were either Eddie Munson, Eleven, Will, and Derek. This was definitely one of the most challenging projects for me and tested not only my creative ability and patience but also my memory and problem solving skills. There were many errors and tribulations throughout the project but it ended up more successful than I thought it would be. I constantly had to see if my results would actually vary through test subjects and just entering differing answers. This really tested my patience. This was honestly a project that opened my eyes the most on challenge, creativity, and constant trial and error. Which led to major growth throughout my journey of becoming an amazing computer scientist.
